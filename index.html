Regional Breakdown · 2025–2026
🌎North America62%
North America leads global enterprise AI adoption at 62%, seven percentage points ahead of Asia (BytePlus 2025)
US private AI investment reached $109.1B in 2024 — nearly 12× China's $9.3B
Canada ranks #5 globally for startup ecosystem strength; US hosts 32 of global top-100 startup cities
Microsoft Copilot adoption among M365 enterprise customers reached 41% by Q1 2026
US ranks #28 for population-level AI usage (41%) despite housing most major AI firms
Sources: AIPRM ↗ · Stanford AI Index ↗
🌍Europe55%
UK leads Western economies with 55% AI adoption, ranking #6 globally; 9 of top 20 countries for AI adoption are European
Baltic nations lead: Latvia #4 (55%), Lithuania #7 (52%), Estonia #8 (51%)
70% of top-10 most AI-prepared countries globally are European
EU AI Act came into force 2024 — driving governance policies; 52% of EU enterprises now have formal AI governance
UK private AI investment: $4.5B in 2024; France's Station F hosts 1,000+ startups across 30 programmes
Sources: Cybernews AI Index ↗ · AIPRM ↗
🌏Asia-Pacific55%
Singapore leads globally with 66% adoption (#1); UAE 56% (#3); China 58% enterprise deployment; India 57%
Asia-Pacific expected to grow at highest CAGR of 19.8% through 2034 — fastest-growing AI region
South Korea made the biggest jump of any country in H2 2025, rising 7 places to #18 globally
India home to 100,000+ recognised startups; Bangalore ranks #10 globally for startup ecosystems
Japan surprisingly low at 17% — despite being a technology leader; cultural and language barriers cited
Sources: AllAboutAI ↗ · Rest of World ↗
🌍Africa~12%
Africa dominates list of least AI-prepared countries — 11 of 12 lowest-scoring nations are African (AIPRM 2025)
DeepSeek usage in Africa is estimated 2–4× higher than other regions due to free access removing cost barriers
Nigeria and Kenya lead African AI adoption; iHub (Nairobi) and CcHub (Lagos) are continent-leading innovation hubs
Huawei and Chinese tech firms actively promoting AI infrastructure across African markets
Growing mobile-first AI adoption driven by smartphone penetration rather than enterprise deployment
Sources: Microsoft AI Diffusion Report ↗
🌎South America~28%
Chile ranks #2 globally for population-level AI adoption at 60% — the only South American country in the global top 20
Brazil has the region's largest startup ecosystem, led by São Paulo; active government AI investment programmes
Venezuela has one of the world's lowest AI adoption rates at 7%, alongside Nigeria and Poland
Latin America adoption growth is hampered by digital infrastructure gaps and limited local-language AI models
Argentina and Colombia emerging as secondary startup hubs, particularly in fintech AI
Sources: Cybernews AI Index ↗
🌏Oceania~35%
Australia and New Zealand both rank in global top 20 for AI adoption; Australia is #8 globally for startup ecosystems
Australia ranks in top 10 AI-prepared nations; government invested AU$2B in national AI strategy 2024
New Zealand is the only non-European, non-Asian country in the global top 10 for AI preparedness
Sydney and Melbourne lead Pacific AI adoption; Stone & Chalk and Cicada Innovations are flagship hubs
Strong growth in AI for agriculture, mining and resource management — unique regional use cases
Sources: AIPRM ↗ · Visual Capitalist ↗
🌐Middle East & Central Asia56%
UAE leads at 56% AI adoption (#3 globally) and 64% in some measurements; government has national AI strategy targeting 2031
Saudi Arabia's NEOM and Vision 2030 are among the world's largest AI infrastructure investment programmes
Dubai's DIFC and Abu Dhabi's Hub71 are top-ranked Middle East incubation ecosystems
AI investment in the Gulf reaching $100B+ through sovereign wealth fund commitments
Sources: AllAboutAI ↗ · PwC AI Research 2025
🧊AntarcticaN/A
No permanent civilian population — AI adoption metrics not applicable in the traditional sense
AI is actively used in Antarctic research: climate modelling, satellite image analysis for ice sheet monitoring
British Antarctic Survey, NASA, and ESA deploy AI for environmental data processing from the continent
Remote sensing AI helps track ice loss, wildlife populations, and atmospheric changes in real time
Sources: British Antarctic Survey · NASA Earth Observatory
